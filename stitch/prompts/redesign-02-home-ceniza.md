Design the HOME PAGE of "AOTR Values" in the LIGHT THEME called "Ceniza", a premium trading-price platform for the game "Attack on Titan Revolution" (Roblox). Desktop 1440px, high-fidelity.

## CONTEXT
Users look up item prices from 2 sources: an OFFICIAL price list (branded GREEN) and a TRADE price list (branded BLUE). Every item shows 3 currencies: keys (🔑), scrolls (📜) and vizard (🎭). This is the LIGHT variant of the existing dark design system (lavender primary #cfbcff, gold accent #e7c365, Space Grotesk headings, Inter body, JetBrains Mono data).

Warm, ember-and-ash inspired light theme from Attack on Titan: cream/stone warm surfaces (like #f7f3ee / #efe9df), warm off-white cards, dark warm ink text (#1c1917-ish), with ember RED and burnt ORANGE accents (like #dc2626 / #ea580c) for primary actions and highlights, warm gold touches. Soft shadows, warm borders. Cozy, editorial, high-contrast and perfectly readable. State the hex values you use.
